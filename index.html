  • Meal Checker: free for organizations

I build software that people can rely on. My work includes healthcare systems and tools for communities that larger technology companies often overlook. Every project starts with understanding the problem. I define what the software needs to do and test whether it works before releasing it.

My Clinical RAG Assistant answers medical questions using WHO guidelines. It cites the sources behind its answers and makes clear when it cannot find enough evidence. My research at York University uses diffusion models to change one medical finding in a chest X-ray while keeping the rest of the image unchanged. This helps determine whether AI models recognize actual medical findings or rely on misleading patterns. I am completing an MSc in AI and Computer Science at the University of Calabria.

Meal Checker replaces paper meal records with a mobile and web application. Staff scan automatically refreshing QR codes to record meals and prevent duplicate collection. Administrators can track meals in real time and export reports. I built it with Flutter, Supabase and PostgreSQL. I am willing to make it available free of charge to organizations that serve meals to their communities.

Tigrigna Keyboard is a native Android and iOS keyboard that supports Tigrigna and English typing. It also uses Google’s ML Kit to recognize handwritten Tigrigna characters. Eleven people are currently testing it. I built it to make typing in Tigrigna easier and more accessible.

I want my work to solve real problems for people who depend on it.
